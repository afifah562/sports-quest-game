# Teacher Manual — The Ultimate Sports Quest

**Class:** Year 4 English (ESL) · **Unit:** Get Smart Plus 4, Unit 9 — Get Active! · **Time:** about 30 minutes (provisional)

> **Before you teach:** The learning objectives below are **provisional** and need your confirmation. DSKP learning standards are **pending verification** and are not written into the game.

## Provisional learning objectives

By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to:
1. Identify different sports and their equipment.
2. Use superlative adjectives correctly (tall → tallest, easy → easiest, good → best, bad → worst, difficult → most difficult, popular → most popular).
3. Create simple sentences to talk about sports.

## Physical learning kit (used with the game)

| Kit item | Used in | What pupils do |
|---|---|---|
| Sports Fact File | Checkpoint 1 | Look up clues to identify each sport |
| Sentence Cards | Checkpoint 3 | Take the card that matches the sentence on screen |
| Decoder Wheel | Checkpoint 3 | Spin it to find the superlative before typing |
| Microphone prop | Checkpoint 5 | Hold it while reading the sports report aloud |

*The game tells pupils when to use each item. It cannot check that they used it; you or the group leader confirm this.*

## Before the lesson (about 10 minutes)

1. Open the game on each device (laptop or tablet, in Chrome, Edge or Safari).
2. Press **👩‍🏫 Teacher** on the welcome screen. Check the **asset list**:
   - ✅ Found = your file is working.
   - ❌ Missing = a placeholder or "NOT YET AVAILABLE" message will show.
3. Test sound: press **🔊 Sound** and **🎵 Music**. The browser only plays sound after a tap.
4. If a previous group used the device, press **↺ Reset** (in the top bar or the Teacher Panel).
5. Put the physical kit beside each device.

## Lesson flow (about 30 minutes)

| Time | Stage | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Welcome + teams | Pupils press **Start Adventure** and choose a team. |
| 3–5 min | Objectives | Read the three goals together. Press **Listen** (if the narration is added). Pupils tap each goal. |
| 5–9 min | CP1 Sports Detective | Pupils flip the six Mystery Cards, read the clues, use the Sports Fact File and choose the sport. |
| 9–12 min | CP2 Equipment Match | Pupils drag (or tap, then tap) the equipment onto the correct sport. |
| 12–17 min | CP3 Grammar Decoder | Pupils take a Sentence Card, spin the Decoder Wheel, press **"I have spun my Decoder Wheel"** and type the superlative. |
| 17–20 min | CP4 Virtual Referee | Play the replay video (if added), then press **Start** for six timed questions (20 seconds each). |
| 20–26 min | CP5 Sports Reporter | Pupils build a report, check it, practise with the microphone prop and read it aloud. **You press "Teacher: confirm speaking".** |
| 26–30 min | CP6 Champion Zone | Five final questions, then badges, a reflection and the certificate. |

## Checkpoint guidance

**Checkpoint 1 — Sports Detective.** Six cards: badminton, football, swimming, cycling, basketball and ice hockey. Wrong choices are greyed out, and the coach encourages the pupil to try again.

**Checkpoint 2 — Equipment Match.** Racket and shuttlecock → badminton · football → football · goggles → swimming · bicycle and helmet → cycling · basketball → basketball · hockey stick and puck → ice hockey. Drag works with a mouse and touch. If dragging is hard, pupils can **tap the equipment, then tap the sport**. Keyboard users can do the same with Tab and Enter.

**Checkpoint 3 — Grammar Decoder.** Seven sentences in order: tallest, fastest, easiest, best, worst, most difficult, most popular. Answers are accepted in any capitals, with extra spaces, and with or without "the". After a wrong answer, a rule hint appears. **📒 Decoder Notes** (tap-to-flip rule cards) are on the left.

**Checkpoint 4 — Virtual Referee.** A game show with a referee, whistle, 20-second countdown and scoreboard. If time runs out, the question restarts and counts as a retry (5 points). The replay screen has Play, Pause, Replay, a progress bar and volume. *If no MP4 is added, it shows "VIDEO NOT YET AVAILABLE".*

**Checkpoint 5 — Sports Reporter.** Pupils choose a sport, a superlative and a reason, then press **Put it in my report**. They can edit the text. **Check my report** looks for a sport and a superlative such as "the most exciting". Pupils then read aloud. **You confirm the speaking** with the purple button. This is the only way to finish Checkpoint 5.
- *Optional recording:* pupils can record and play back their voice if the browser supports it and they allow the microphone. The recording stays on the device and is never uploaded. **There is no speech recognition**; your judgement is the assessment.

**Checkpoint 6 — Champion Zone.** Five questions (vocabulary, equipment, superlatives, sentence completion and reading), each with instant feedback. Then pupils see their total points, six badges, a trophy and confetti. They type a reflection ("What did you learn today?") and their name, and press **Print certificate**.

## Assessment notes

- **Formative:** instant feedback on every answer. Points show whether an answer was right first time (10) or after a retry (5).
- **Evidence:** the Teacher Panel shows the pupil's points, stars, completed checkpoints and reflection.
- **Speaking:** teacher observation plus the confirmation button.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No sound | Tap the screen first; check 🔊 Sound is ON; check the device volume. |
| "AUDIO NOT YET AVAILABLE" | The MP3 is missing or misnamed. See `assets/audio/ADD_AUDIO_HERE.txt`. |
| "VIDEO NOT YET AVAILABLE" | Add `assets/video/referee-replay.mp4`. |
| Placeholder images | Add PNGs with the exact names in `assets/images/ADD_IMAGES_HERE.txt`. |
| Markers don't line up with my board | Edit `pos: [x, y]` (percentages) in `CHECKPOINTS` in `script.js`. |
| Progress not saving | Private or incognito browsing can block saving. Use a normal window. |
| New pupil on the same device | Press ↺ Reset. |
